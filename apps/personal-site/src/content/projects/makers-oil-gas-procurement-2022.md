## Problem

Procurement in the oil and gas sector crosses operators, vendors, and approvers, and each party keeps its own version of how purchases move. Without one documented process, every handoff becomes a dispute about what was agreed.

## Approach

Project at Makers Institute. Involved as Business Process Designer. Identified and documented the procurement business processes within the oil and gas sector, fixing the as-is flow so builders and reviewers share one reference.

## Result

The engagement works from one documented procurement process instead of competing informal versions.

Project at Makers Institute, 2022. Internal deliverable, no public listing.
