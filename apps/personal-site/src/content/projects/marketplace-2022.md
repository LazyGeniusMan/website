## Problem

A B2C marketplace startup records orders, commissions, payouts, and refunds across buyers, sellers, and the operator. Without a chart of accounts and accounting treatment agreed up front, every transaction category becomes a later reconciliation dispute.

## Approach

As Business Process Designer, I prepared detailed business process documentation, chart of accounts, and accounting treatment for a B2C marketplace startup, so the marketplace system posts each transaction type to an account defined before the build.

## Result

The marketplace operates on one documented chart of accounts with accounting treatments its builders and accountants share.

Project at Makers Institute, 2022. Internal deliverable, no public listing.
