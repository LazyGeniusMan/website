## Problem

The Center for Digital Innovation Studies (DIGITS) Unpad needed its website set up on a maintainable platform instead of whatever came before. DIGITS is a Faculty of Economics and Business Unpad research center sustaining a diginnovation ecosystem.

## Approach

Project at Center for Digital Innovation Studies (DIGITS) Unpad. Involved as website admin. Setup and migrated to Wordpress in Web Hosting Platform. This matches experience/digits-2020.json (Research Assistant: migrated, redesigned, and maintained institutional websites; supervision of a website administration intern).

## Result

The center's site runs on WordPress in its web hosting platform, ready for the redesign work that followed in 2022.
