## Problem

A B2B tour and travel company moves money across agents, operators, and travelers, and each booking type needs an accounting treatment before developers can build it. Without a chart of accounts agreed up front, the application and the ledger diverge.

## Approach

Project at Makers Institute, continued at PT Navcore Nextology. Involved as Business Process Designer. Designed wireframes, charts of accounts, and accounting treatment for the B2B tour and travel company, then facilitated the alignment of the tour applications with business processes and requirements through comprehensive test cases.

## Result

Tour bookings flow through tested application paths into accounts defined before the build.

Project at Makers Institute into PT Navcore Nextology, 2023. Internal deliverable, no public listing.
