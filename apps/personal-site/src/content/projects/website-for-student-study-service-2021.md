## Problem

The Student Study Service project at Universitas Padjadjaran needed a website where its study material and updates could be published without hand-built pages.

## Approach

Project when Student Study Service at Universitas Padjadjaran. Involved as website admin. Setup and designed in Ghost Hosting Platform. This matches experience/sss-2021.json (Website administrator: Ghost CMS, educational content about fishery products for SMEs, catalog design, Maps onboarding).

## Result

The study service publishes through its own site on the Ghost hosting platform, maintained by the project's website admin. The site stayed live on Ghost hosting after the January–February 2021 role ended, with later educational posts added as post-role continuation.
