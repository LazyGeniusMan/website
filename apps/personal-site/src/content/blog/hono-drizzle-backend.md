As a freelance Business Process and Enterprise System Specialist, I carry engagements from requirements to a running service: functional requirements from project goals, REST API backends in Hono with Drizzle ORM, third-party integrations, and GitLab CI and CD deploying to Cloudflare infrastructure. The land arrangement booking platform at [makadinah.id](https://makadinah.id) and the Office AI backend at [kinaia.app](https://kinaia.app) both shipped this way.

## Process model before endpoints

Every engagement starts where the analyst years taught me to start: the business process model and the information system design. Endpoints then mirror real events, bookings, confirmations, notifications, instead of CRUD guesses, and integrations with ERP, identity, notification, scheduler, and search services plug into defined steps.

## Small stack, automated delivery

Hono keeps the runtime light, Drizzle keeps the schema explicit, and the CI pipeline deploys to Workers, D1, R2, KV, Queue, and Workflow on every merge. Training sessions hand the running system to clients who understand the process it enacts, because they helped define it.

## Freelance is initiation to training

Scoping man-hours with partners, negotiating deliverables, modeling, building, deploying, training: the whole arc is one person's responsibility. That constraint is clarifying. Nothing survives that is not required, and everything required is written down first.
