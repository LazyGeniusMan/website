## Problem

Core banking and loan origination systems decide who gets money and how it is tracked, so their requirements must be written before configuration starts. A lending build without functional requirements bakes policy ambiguity into the ledger.

## Approach

Project at PT Navcore Nextology. Involved as Business Process Designer. Created functional requirements for Core Banking and Loan Origination Systems, in the team's BPMN and DMN modeling practice with ERP integration boundaries considered from the start.

## Result

The banking and origination builds proceed from written functional requirements that reviewers and developers share.

Project at PT Navcore Nextology, 2024. Internal deliverable, no public listing.
