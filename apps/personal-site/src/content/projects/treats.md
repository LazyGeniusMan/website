## Problem

An online food delivery app needs more than buyer and seller screens: someone has to define how orders, preparation, handover, and settlement move between parties before any screen can enact them.

## Approach

Project at PT Navcore Nextology. Involved as Business Process Designer for Online Food Delivery Apps. Designed end to end operating model and business process model, so the buyer and seller apps implement one shared process instead of two disconnected views.

## Result

The delivery operation runs on a defined operating model carried by the buyer and seller apps. TREats (TokRum Eats) ships as companion local food apps: the buyer app orders from neighborhood sellers and the seller app fulfills those orders under the same process.
