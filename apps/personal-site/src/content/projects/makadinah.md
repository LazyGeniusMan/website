## Problem

Booking land arrangement services ran on informal messages between parties, with no shared definition of what a booking contains or when it is confirmed.

## Approach

Project as Freelancer. Involved as Business Process Designer for Land Arrangement Booking Platform. Designed business process model and information systems then implemented, deployed and maintained backend services in VPS, carrying the engagement from requirements through a running service.

## Result

Bookings run on defined requirements enacted by a maintained backend service, replacing ad-hoc coordination with a process both sides can see.
