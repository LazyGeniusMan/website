## Problem

A crowdfunding platform moves money between funders, fundraisers, and the operator, and each movement needs an accounting treatment before developers can build it. Without written functional requirements, the build encodes guesses about fund flows.

## Approach

As Business Process Designer, I developed functional requirements and accounting treatments for a Crowdfunding platform, working from the operating-model practice of the team: BPMN and DMN models plus REST API boundaries, with UI/UX alignment checked against the requirements.

## Result

The crowdfunding build proceeds from defined functional requirements and accounting treatments rather than from assumed fund flows.

Project at PT Navcore Nextology, 2024. Internal deliverable, no public listing.
