## Problem

Group bookings cross several parties (customers, organizers, and providers), and each party kept its own informal procedure. Requests fell through the gaps between them.

## Approach

Project as Freelancer. Involved as Business Process Designer for Group Booking Platform. Designed business process model and information systems then implemented, deployed and maintained backend services in VPS: the process model came first, the system and its services were built to enact it.

## Result

Group bookings now follow one modeled process from request to confirmation, enacted by backend services that were designed from that model rather than fitted to it afterward.
