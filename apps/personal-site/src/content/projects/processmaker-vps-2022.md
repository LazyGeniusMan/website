## Problem

Teaching process automation needs every student working against a live engine, but a single shared instance means one student's experiment breaks the class. Without isolated instances on budgeted infrastructure, coursework stays theoretical.

## Approach

As Teaching Assistant for the Business Process Management course, I prepared written and visual materials on process automation with ProcessMaker, budgeted a cost-effective cloud virtual private server, then set up, safeguarded, and maintained the VPS for multi-instance ProcessMaker deployment with Docker containers, and supervised the following assistantship's automation projects and server administration.

## Result

Students execute business process automation projects on maintained instances instead of reading about automation.

Project at FEB Unpad, 2022 into 2023. Internal teaching infrastructure, no public listing.
