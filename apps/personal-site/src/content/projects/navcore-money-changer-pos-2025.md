## Problem

A money changer point of sales prices currencies, settles cash, and posts each trade to the ledger in one counter interaction. Without functional requirements and accounting treatments written first, rate handling and settlement encode teller-by-teller guesses.

## Approach

Project at PT Navcore Nextology. Involved as Business Process Designer. Developed functional requirements, accounting treatments, and wireframe for a money changer point of sales, in the team's BPMN and DMN modeling practice with UI/UX alignment checked against the requirements.

## Result

The point of sales build proceeds from defined requirements and treatments rather than from assumed counter flows.

Project at PT Navcore Nextology, 2025. Internal deliverable, no public listing.
