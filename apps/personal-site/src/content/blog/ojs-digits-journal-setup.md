At the Center for Digital Innovation Studies (DIGITS) Unpad, the Journal of Digital Innovation Studies needed a publishing home: submissions, review, and published issues in one system instead of scattered files.

## One system for the editorial flow

I set up and administered Open Journal Systems on the faculty web hosting, designing the journal site so authors, reviewers, and readers each saw the step meant for them. The journal runs open-access under E-ISSN 2829-6214, with its first volume dated 2022.

## Research support around the platform

The same engagement covered Grounded Theory processing of blockchain research data, survey questionnaires, Google Data Studio visualization, and webinar support. The OJS work was the visible artifact; the research pipeline behind it kept the content coming.

## Migration discipline reused

Setting up the journal alongside the [DIGITS website migration](/experience/digits-2020) taught a durable lesson: migrate content with its URLs and ownership intact, then redesign. The 2020 WordPress setup and the 2022 redesign of the center site followed that same sequence.
