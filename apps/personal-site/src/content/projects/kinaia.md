## Problem

An AI assistant for Microsoft Office Suite is only useful if it answers from the user's own documents and stays available while documents change. A demo that reads static files does not survive contact with real office work.

## Approach

Project as Freelancer. Involved as Backend Engineer for Microsoft Office Suite AI Assistant. Designed, implemented, deployed and maintained backend services in VPS: service boundaries around document ingestion and answering, integrations with the surrounding systems, and deployment automation so releases are routine.

## Result

The assistant runs as maintained backend services on a VPS rather than a demo on a laptop. Office users interact with it where they already work, backed by services with defined responsibilities and repeatable deploys.
