As website administrator for the Student Study Service at Universitas Padjadjaran, I set up a Ghost-hosted site so fishery SMEs had somewhere to publish educational content about fishery products without hand-built pages.

## Content the SMEs could use

The work was editorial as much as technical: posts on fishery products for small producers, plus a designed catalog they could share with buyers. Social content pointed back to the same site, so one publishing flow served both channels.

## Found on the map

Many SMEs were invisible on search, so part of the engagement was helping them register their locations on Google Maps. A buyer who read a post could then find the producer.

## Post-role continuation

The January–February 2021 role set the site up; the site stayed live on Ghost hosting after the role ended, with later educational posts added as continuation. The same Ghost and content workflow carried into later [DIGITS](/experience/digits-2020) research-support work, where publishing flow mattered as much as the platform.
