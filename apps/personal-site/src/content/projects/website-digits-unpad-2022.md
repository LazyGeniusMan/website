## Problem

The Center for Digital Innovation Studies (DIGITS) Unpad needed its WordPress website redesigned to present the center's work clearly to visitors. DIGITS is a Faculty of Economics and Business Unpad research center sustaining a diginnovation ecosystem, so the redesign carries faculty research visibility, not just a theme refresh.

## Approach

As website admin, I redesigned Wordpress Website on the center's web hosting platform.

## Result

The redesigned site presents DIGITS Unpad on its own domain, maintained by the center's website admin.
