## Problem

Booking of land arrangement services ran on informal and manual communication between parties, with no shared definition of what a booking contains, how much the price, and when the timelimit is.

## Approach

As Business Process Designer, I designed business process model and backend services then implemented, deployed and maintained it in VPS so land arrangement services that booking flow is automated end to end from product definition to order completion.

## Result

Bookings flow run on defined products, pricing, and rules in the dashboard. Notification and invoicing are automated through email and whatsapp, replacing ad-hoc coordination with a process both sides can see.
