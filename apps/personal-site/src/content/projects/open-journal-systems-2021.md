## Problem

The Journal of Digital Innovation Studies needed a publishing platform where submissions, review, and published issues live in one system instead of scattered files.

## Approach

Project at Center for Digital Innovation Studies (DIGITS) Unpad. Involved as website admin. Setup and designed Open Journal Systems (OJS) in Web Hosting Platform. Screenshot of the OJS homepage above.

## Result

The journal publishes through its own Open Journal Systems installation on the faculty's web hosting. It runs as an open-access journal under E-ISSN 2829-6214, with its first volume dated 2022.
